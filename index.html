How the Data Is Dated

/ 01

Point-in-time availability

Where disclosure timing is known, market cap and similar derived fields use the share capital figure that was actually available as of each historical date, rather than the figure known today.

/ 02

Documented fallback logic

When disclosure timing data is missing or inconsistent, we fall back to statutory filing deadlines. The full rule set is documented, not hidden.

/ 03

Backward-adjusted pricing

Adjusted close prices are anchored to the most recent trading date in each dataset and adjusted backward through dividend events.
