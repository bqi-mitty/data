How the Data Is Dated

/ 01

Availability-based dating

For tables under active monitoring, timestamps are intended to reflect when the source information became available. If a record had to be reconstructed later — for example after a collection gap — the timestamp may instead reflect when the figure entered our database. The dating approach is documented by dataset.

/ 02

Fallback rules when timing is unavailable

When disclosure timing is missing or inconsistent, we may use statutory filing deadlines as a fallback. The applicable rule is documented for the dataset.

Structured, cross-referenced, and timestamped through our point-in-time capture pipeline. Data may contain errors, gaps, or delays. Provided as-is, for research and reference purposes.

FAQ

What is point-in-time data, and why does it matter?
Point-in-time data aims to use the information that was available as of each historical date. For example, market capitalization can be calculated using the share-capital figure available at that time rather than a later value. This helps reduce look-ahead bias in backtests.
What markets and time ranges do you cover?
Taiwan (TWSE), with additional Japan and US datasets available. See the Market Coverage section above for exact date ranges per data type.
What's the difference between Free, the Trial, and the Paid Subscription?
Free provides daily OHLCV for a small set of major TWSE-listed companies. The 3-Month Trial adds broader TWSE-listed coverage, including balance sheet and income statement data, dividends, stock splits, and point-in-time dating for 90 days, but does not include a historical dataset. Cash flow statement data is not currently included. The Paid Subscription continues the same feed while your subscription remains active.
Do I need a credit card for the Free plan or the Trial?
No. Both Free and the 3-Month Trial only require an email address. Card details are only needed if you subscribe to a paid plan.
What happens when my trial ends?
Delivery simply stops after 90 days — you won't be automatically charged. If you'd like to keep receiving data, subscribe to the Paid plan whenever you're ready. Each email address is eligible for one trial.
How is the data delivered?
By email as CSV files, typically within an 18:00–23:00 (UTC+8) delivery window on applicable trading days. No API key or system integration is required.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, subscriptions can be cancelled anytime through your Polar customer portal, effective at the end of the current billing period. Any data already delivered to you is yours to keep; cancelling only stops future deliveries.
What if a data point I already received turns out to be wrong or gets revised?
For the Trial and Paid Subscription, corrected data is re-sent automatically when source figures are revised — same trading day for closing prices, within 30 days of original delivery for dividends and stock splits, and within the same fiscal quarter for balance sheet and income statement items. Outside those windows, or if you spot something that looks wrong, email us at bqi.mitty@gmail.com with the ticker and date and we'll investigate.
Can I resell or redistribute the data?
No — data is licensed for internal research use only. Redistribution or resale of raw data requires written permission.
Is this investment advice?
No. Data is provided for research and reference purposes only.
Do you offer custom datasets (different markets, fields, or history)?
Custom datasets may be available depending on the requested market, fields, and history. See the Extended / Custom plan or email us to discuss your requirements.
