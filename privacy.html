Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BQI Data ("we", "us"), operated by Shaochieh Hu, collects when you visit our website, contact us, or purchase our data products, and how we use and handle that information.

1. Information we collect

We may receive information in the following ways:

  • Purchases and subscriptions: Checkout is handled by Polar (polar.sh), our merchant of record. Polar collects your payment details directly. We receive the email address and order information needed to deliver your purchase and provide support. We do not receive or store your payment card number.
  • When you contact us: If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details of your inquiry.
  • Website usage: We use Google Analytics to understand basic site usage, such as pages viewed and how visitors reach the site. See Section 6 for more information.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver purchased datasets and daily subscription feeds by email;
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support;
  • To investigate and correct reported data issues;
  • To meet legal and tax obligations related to processing payments;
  • To understand overall website traffic and usage, via Google Analytics.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your contact information to third parties.

3. Third parties

Polar acts as our payment processor and merchant of record and processes your payment and billing information under its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Polar's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data. We use Brevo for email delivery of purchased datasets. We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to measure website traffic and usage; see Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google processes this data.

4. Data retention

We keep order and correspondence records for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Service, handle support requests, and meet accounting or legal requirements.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing bqi.mitty@gmail.com. If your inquiry relates to payment information, we may direct you to Polar, who holds that data on our behalf.

Some historical datasets we distribute, such as director and supervisor shareholding records, are compiled from information publicly disclosed by Taiwan-listed companies. If your name appears in this data and you would like us to review a removal request, please contact us.

6. Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics, deployed via Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use our website — for example, which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive at the site. These services may set cookies in your browser to collect this usage information. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not combine it with your order or subscription information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's browser add-on or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings. Any session information used at checkout is handled by Polar under its own policy.

7. International users

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or elsewhere with data protection laws that grant you additional rights, you may exercise those rights by contacting us at the email above.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, individuals under the age of 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to bqi.mitty@gmail.com.
