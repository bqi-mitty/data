Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BQI Data ("we", "us"), operated by Shaochieh Hu, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or purchase our data products.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Purchases and subscriptions: When you buy a sample or subscribe to a plan, checkout is processed by Polar (polar.sh), our merchant of record. Polar collects your email address and payment details directly; we receive your email address and order information from Polar in order to deliver your data and provide support. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Direct contact: If you email us directly, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message (typically your email address and inquiry).

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver purchased datasets and daily subscription feeds by email;
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support;
  • To investigate and correct reported data issues;
  • To meet legal and tax obligations related to processing payments.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your contact information to third parties.

3. Third parties

Polar acts as our payment processor and merchant of record and processes your payment and billing information under its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Polar's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data. We use Brevo for email delivery of purchased datasets.

4. Data retention

We retain order and correspondence records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, respond to support requests, and meet accounting and legal obligations.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing bqi.mitty@gmail.com. If your inquiry relates to payment information, we may direct you to Polar, who holds that data on our behalf.

Some of the historical data we distribute (for example, director and supervisor shareholding records) is compiled from information that Taiwan-listed companies are legally required to disclose publicly. If you are named in this data and would like it removed from our datasets, please contact us and we will review your request.

6. Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Any session information used at checkout is handled by Polar under its own policy.

7. International users

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or elsewhere with data protection laws that grant you additional rights, you may exercise those rights by contacting us at the email above.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, individuals under the age of 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to bqi.mitty@gmail.com.
